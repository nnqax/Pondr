# 🦆 Pondr — Your AI Productivity & Pacing Partner

> *Glide through your day like a duck on still water.*

Pondr is a human-centric, calm productivity app designed to eliminate task paralysis, overbooking, and guilt-driven to-do lists. Powered by an interactive Hugging Face AI chatbot assistant (Duckley), Pondr automatically paces your workload into balanced focus blocks and gentle task deferrals.

### ✨ Key Features
* 💬 **Smart Conversational Onboarding:** Talk naturally to Duckley to map out your day based on your actual energy levels.
* 🐥 **Duckling Steps (Subtask Breakdown):** Deconstruct overwhelming projects into bite-sized, doable micro-tasks.
* ⚡ **Paddling vs. Basking Pacing:** Clearly separate intense deep work (**Paddling**) from light admin tasks & recovery (**Basking**).
* 🌊 **"Let It Float" Deferrals:** Seamlessly push non-essential tasks downstream to tomorrow—zero red alerts, zero guilt.
* 🔔 **Interactive Notification Bridge:** Real-time in-page alerts and web notifications keeping you gently on track.
